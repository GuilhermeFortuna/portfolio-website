## Project Stack Contract

- Existing Quant and Nexo Dental entries become large editorial proof cards in
  their existing order, with title, summary, and approved link unchanged.
- Sticky/scale stacking is presentational. Both cards remain ordinary semantic
  articles/links in document order and never cover the focused card.
- No screenshot, logo, metric, testimonial, technology claim, or relation to an
  employer may be invented to fill the composition.
- Mobile, reduced motion, no-JS, 200% zoom, and short viewports use a spaced
  static article list with no sticky overlap.

## Convergence Contract

- Adapt the Gemini path concept as restrained SVG/CSS linework using existing
  accent and line tokens. It is decorative, `aria-hidden`, and non-WebGL.
- Paths may represent the established chapters converging, but labels must use
  existing localized section/action language rather than new claims.
- The visual endpoint is the existing View PDF, Download PDF, and email/contact
  actions. All actions remain normal links available before animation/hydration.
- Education and language content sits in normal flow before the convergence and
  is not forced into cards, orbit nodes, or animated paths.
- Reduced/static modes show a composed still or omit the decorative paths while
  retaining the complete closing content and actions.
